Control and planning

A3Pure Pursuit look-ahead前视距离试驾场Short look-ahead weaves, long look-ahead cuts corners: 1.23 / 0.27 / 0 m overshoot at 2 / 5 / 15 m. A7Lane change, slowed downS 形变道慢放A quintic (minimum-jerk) lane change vs a straight diagonal at 10 m/s: steering wheel, lateral acceleration and d / speed / acceleration curves in sync. Left, centre, right, centre; half the time means 4x the lateral acceleration. A7Why plan in Frenet: a lane change in a curve弯道变道两种画法Same left curve, same Pure Pursuit. Offsetting the path toward the road's left (Frenet) ends in the left lane; adding d to map y ends back in the own lane (3.50 m off) once the road points up. Lane change plus curve peaks at 3.19 m/s². A3 · metricLateral error, done right离中心线多远Distance to the nearest waypoint hides up to 0.25 m of error; point-to-segment distance fixes it. A8Controller lag changes safety metrics刹车慢半拍Same planner, lagging PID: minimum gap drops from 4.96 m to 4.36 m. A8Car following in a curve弯道跟车：两种开法IDM + Pure Pursuit + bicycle model, with the lead car braking inside the curve. A8Stop-and-hold with hysteresis停车保持的门槛Different enter and exit thresholds stop the creep-stop-creep cycle at low speed.

Tracking

A6Kalman filter: predict, then measure卡尔曼：猜一步，量一下One frame of a 1D Kalman filter on a distance number line: predict, measure, blend by confidence. On a frame where differencing reads −7.5 m/s, the filter only nudges its speed from −1.81 to −1.97. A6Multi-object tracking: who is who多车跟踪：谁是谁Three cars, radar boxes with no names in shuffled order. Each frame: predict every track, match by a distance table and the Hungarian algorithm with a 2 m gate, then manage IDs. Includes a 5-frame occlusion (keep 10 frames: same ID; keep 3: the car comes back as a new ID) and a guardrail false alarm that lives 1.1 s as a ghost track.
